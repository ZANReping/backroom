# Level 1 texture sources

The seven `l1/*.png` textures are generated deterministically by `scripts/gen-l1-materials.mjs`. Foliage and data maps are procedural; peeling paint combines the existing CC0 concrete texture with original procedural paint remnants.

The reused `l11_concrete`, `l11_plaster`, and `l11_metal` materials derive from the existing [L11 asset sources](../L11-ASSET-SOURCES.md) and use CC0 ambientCG material sources.

Five user references and the Backrooms wiki informed the visual design only. They are design references; no image from those references is redistributed here.

Peeling paint uses the existing CC0 `l11_concrete.jpg` as its base, with original procedural paint remnants and scratches layered on top.
