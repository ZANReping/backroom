# Level 11 texture sources

All seven sets are sourced from ambientCG's official asset pages and downloaded as their 1K JPG packages:

- [Concrete034](https://ambientcg.com/view?id=Concrete034) → `l11_concrete`
- [Asphalt012](https://ambientcg.com/view?id=Asphalt012) → `l11_asphalt`
- [Bricks097](https://ambientcg.com/view?id=Bricks097) → `l11_brick`
- [Plaster004](https://ambientcg.com/view?id=Plaster004) → `l11_plaster`
- [Tiles074](https://ambientcg.com/view?id=Tiles074) → `l11_tiles`
- [Metal032](https://ambientcg.com/view?id=Metal032) → `l11_metal`
- [WoodFloor051](https://ambientcg.com/view?id=WoodFloor051) → `l11_wood`

Download endpoint: `https://ambientcg.com/get?file=ASSET_1K-JPG.zip` (replace `ASSET` with the asset ID above). Assets are CC0; see the [ambientCG license](https://docs.ambientcg.com/license/).

Map convention: `*.jpg` Color maps are interpreted as sRGB; `*_normal.jpg` and `*_roughness.jpg` are data maps and must be interpreted in linear space.
